Escape from the Crater's Heart

Taro flees the blacklight depths of Crater City as its megastructure trembles. Racing through neon-scarred corridors and fractured districts, he carries a fragment of forbidden knowledge—one that demands silence or death. The Crater does not release its secrets willingly.

Crater City's throat descends in fractals—each level a confession.
Perfect geometry fails. COHESIVA bonds writhe purple. The infrastructure knows.
Above: clinical time. Below: the sound of a city rejecting them.
Efficiency demands sacrifice. Sacrifice demands descent.
Reality folds. The body remains in two places at once.
Spatia tears through consciousness—indifferent. Unasked. Inevitable.
Gate-Sickness: the price written in neurons. The folds never leave.
They ache like phantom limbs before the storm.
Crater City's foundation. The price of vertical perfection.
Millions of Spectres—shattered, unfused, imprisoned in purple dark. Dying without death.
The tar breathes. A mass grave that feeds on horror.
They stand inside a wound that refuses to close.
The Slag claims all that dies. Taro remembers what it means to live.
Energia: sovereign demolition. Sovereign repair.
One bulb. One act of will. The darkness knows it has company.
The price of channeling such force is written in the body's debt.
Space folds like paper. The corridor compresses to a doorway.
Distance becomes negotiable. The arm extends; the world obeys.
But geometry has limits. The tear widens—absence leaks through.
Reality stitched closed. The angles remain wrong.
The Radiant District—where light learns to die.
Root Rot blooms beneath the city's skin. Nova Terra watches. And grieves in silence.
A garden exhales its last luminescence. Taro kneels in the ash of what was promised.
Above: cold calculation. Below: the void writes its name in darkness.
